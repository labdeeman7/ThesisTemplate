# Archived Chapter 5 optimisation sensitivity study

This directory preserves the exploratory Direct SFT optimisation study formerly included in the Chapter 5 supplementary material. It was removed from the active thesis during the author read-through because several optimisation variables changed simultaneously, so the runs do not form a controlled ablation and are not needed to support the chapter's principal scientific claims.

The numerical results and accompanying interpretation are retained verbatim in `optimisation_sensitivity.tex` for project history. They are not part of the authoritative active Chapter 5 narrative.
