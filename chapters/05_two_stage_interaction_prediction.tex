\section*{Chapter Summary and Transition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Chapter Summary and Transition}

This chapter completes the technical progression of the thesis by separating the grounded triplet task into specialist instrument localisation and multimodal interaction prediction. It demonstrates that an MLLM can predict structured verb--target interactions for supplied instrument instances and that the complete two-stage system remains competitive when those instances are produced by the first-stage model. The results also show that the quality of the final grounded triplet depends jointly on the spatial and semantic stages, connecting the strengths and remaining limitations observed throughout Chapters~\ref{chap:cholecinstanceseg}--\ref{chap:two-stage-interaction}.

The following chapter brings these contributions together. It considers what the review, datasets, grounded benchmark, target-aware architecture, and two-stage formulation collectively establish about surgical scene understanding, before discussing the limitations of strict triplet evaluation and the most important directions for extending the work beyond laparoscopic cholecystectomy.
