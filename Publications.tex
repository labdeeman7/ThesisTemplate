\section*{Relationship to Thesis Chapters}

\Chapref{chap:background} draws on the review article, \Chapref{chap:cholecinstanceseg} draws on the CholecInstanceSeg article, and \Chapref{chap:triplet-grounding} draws on the accepted article introducing CholecTriplet-Seg and TargetFusionNet. \section*{Academic Service and Research Engagement}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Co-organiser of the \emph{Large Models Meet Surgical Data Science} (LM-SURG) workshop at the 2026 IEEE International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI), London, UK.

  \item Co-initiated a surgical artificial intelligence collaboration between King's College London and Obafemi Awolowo University Teaching Hospitals Complex (OAUTHC), Nigeria, with Professor Adewale Adisa and Tom Vercauteren, and received support from the King's Global Engagement Partnership Fund to establish a pilot open dataset of Nigerian laparoscopic cholecystectomy videos for surgical safety and scene-understanding research.

  \item Helped establish the collaboration's research infrastructure at OAUTHC by deploying secure local video storage and de-identification workflows, training the surgical team in video-data management, organising a growing multi-procedure laparoscopic video collection, and developing clinical annotation protocols with local collaborators.

  \item Contributed to the organisation of the 2025 conference of the Association of Gynaecological Endoscopic Surgeons of Nigeria (AGES), Lagos, Nigeria.
\end{itemize}
